Where this comes from

The data is built and verified in the type1 repository, where every announcement is one backdated commit, so its history is this timeline. A script replays all {{ br.events | size }} announcements from January 1994 and checks the result against Scryfall’s live list; it reproduces it exactly.

Two things to know before you trust a date

Announcement and effective dates are not the same thing, and a tournament held between them used the old list. Only some announcements record which of the two their date is; the panel says so for each one rather than implying precision that is not there.

The sources disagree on about a dozen dates. Where they do, the cited source wins and the other claim is shown alongside it, flagged disputed in the timeline. The ordering of announcements is reliable; the exact days are still being pinned down.
