After 5 rounds of Swiss, the top 8 played out as so:

## Top 8:
Erin (1) vs Nam (8) – Nam wins.

Randal (2) vs Ben (7) – Ben wins.

Matt (3) vs Duane (6) – Matt wins.

Frank (4) vs Sam (5) – Sam wins.

## Top 4:
Sam vs Nam – Nam wins.

Matt vs Ben – Ben wins.

## Finals:
Ben vs Nam – Nam wins.
