Put photos of your item in this folder.

Rename the two folders above this one:
  your-name  ->  your name, lowercase with dashes, e.g. rajah-james
  your-item  ->  the item, lowercase with dashes, e.g. logo-tee

Name the main photo main.jpg (or main.png) and point the header_img line
in your .md file at it. Simple names, no spaces.

Delete this text file once you've added your photos — it's just a
placeholder so the folder isn't empty in the zip file.
