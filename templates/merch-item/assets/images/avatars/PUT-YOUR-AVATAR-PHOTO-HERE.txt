First time writing for us? Put a small square photo of yourself in this
folder — it shows up next to your name on the post.

Name it something like: your-name_400x400.jpg (a roughly square photo,
at least 400x400 pixels, works best).

Then point to it in the post's front matter:
  author:
    avatar: ../assets/images/avatars/your-name_400x400.jpg

Already written for us before? You can skip this — just reuse the path
to your existing avatar if you know it, or ask and we'll fill it in.

Don't want an avatar photo at all? Delete the whole "avatar:" line from
the front matter instead, and delete this file/folder.
